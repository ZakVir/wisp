## Wisp 2.3.0

**Notes.** Ask Wisp to remember things: "remember that my parking spot is B12", "note that the spare key is in
the blue drawer". Later, ask "what did I ask you to remember?" and Wisp reads them back (newest first, all of
them in a card). "Clear my notes" forgets them.

Notes stay on this Mac (Settings > Privacy shows how many, with **Clear Notes**) and are never sent to the AI.

Requires macOS 15 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon.
