## Wisp 0.9.0

**Keyboard shortcuts card.** Ask "what are the shortcuts here?" (or choose **Keyboard Shortcuts in …** in the
Wisp menu) and Wisp shows every keyboard shortcut of the app you're using, grouped by menu, in a card with a
filter field. Commands that aren't available right now are shown dimmed.

- Read straight from the app's own menus, on your Mac: instant, and nothing is sent anywhere.
- Escape or the × closes it.
- Needs Better answers (Settings > Permissions).

Also more accurate: when an answer points at a button macOS reports as greyed out (and the answer didn't say
so), Wisp adds "… is greyed out right now". In our real-app test, "go back" with nothing to go back to is now
answered correctly.

Requires macOS 15 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon.
