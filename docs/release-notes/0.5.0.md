## Wisp 0.5.0

**Read aloud.** Select some text in any app (an email, an article, a document) and hold the shortcut and say
"read this to me", or choose **Read Selection Aloud** in the Wisp menu. Wisp reads it word for word in its own
voice, with captions.

- It happens entirely on your Mac: nothing is sent anywhere, and it's instant.
- Up to about 8,000 characters at a time; press Escape to stop.
- Needs Better answers (Settings > Permissions), which is how Wisp sees what you've selected.

Requires macOS 15 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon.
