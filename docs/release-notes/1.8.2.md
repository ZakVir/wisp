## Wisp 1.8.2

**Offline? Wisp says so right away.** Without an internet connection, a question gets an immediate answer: Wisp
is offline, and what still works (read aloud, copy text on screen, timers, "say that again"), instead of a long
wait for the AI to time out. The Wisp menu shows "Offline" too. A company AI server on this Mac or local network
keeps working offline.

Requires macOS 15 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon.
