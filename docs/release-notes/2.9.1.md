## Wisp 2.9.1

**A new, smarter model (Kimi K3 was retired).** NVIDIA retired Kimi K3, the model Wisp used for most questions, so
those questions were quietly falling back to the fast model. Wisp now uses the best of NVIDIA's newest free models,
picked by testing them on 58 real questions in real Mac apps:

- **Nemotron 3.5 Super VL** answers everything except quick how-to questions: 94% right (Kimi K3 managed 81%),
  pointing at the right control 98% of the time, and usually talking within about 2 seconds.
- **Step 5 Preview** does drawings ("draw a cat"): it was the only free model whose sketches looked like the thing
  asked for.
- **Nemotron 3 Nano Omni** still answers quick "how do I…" questions in about a second.
- If your settings named Kimi K3, they now use Nemotron 3.5 Super VL automatically.

Also tested and not chosen: DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (didn't reply within 3 minutes) and Nemotron 3.5 Lightning (can't
see the screen, and took over 2 minutes to answer).

Requires macOS 15 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon.
