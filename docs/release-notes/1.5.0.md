## Wisp 1.5.0

**Writing help.** Select text in any app (an email, a message, a document), hold the shortcut and ask for a
change:

- "make this more polite / shorter / more professional / friendlier"
- "fix the grammar", "proofread this"
- "translate this into Spanish"
- "summarize this", "turn this into bullet points"
- "write a reply to this message"

Wisp writes the new version, shows it in a card with **Copy**, and puts it on your clipboard: just paste it over
your text. (Wisp never types into your apps itself.) It's also in History and Copy Last Answer.

Works with the free NVIDIA model (usually about a second). Needs Better answers (Settings > Permissions) to see
your selection; the selected text goes to the AI service with the request.

Requires macOS 15 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon.
