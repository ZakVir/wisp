## Wisp 1.6.0

**Read the page aloud.** With nothing selected, hold the shortcut and say "read this to me", "read this page to
me" or "read my email out loud": Wisp reads the text of the window in front, top to bottom, in its own voice with
captions. Press Escape to stop.

- Only that window is read, even if other windows overlap it.
- Done on your Mac with macOS's text recognition: nothing is sent anywhere. Private apps are never read.
- With text selected, Wisp reads just the selection, as before.

Requires macOS 15 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon.
