## Wisp 2.6.0

**Wisp draws on your screen.** Ask it to draw something and the cursor really draws it, stroke by stroke,
around your pointer:

- Shapes, instantly: "draw a star", "draw a heart", "draw a smiley face", circle, square, triangle, check mark,
  arrow, spiral, house.
- Anything else: "draw a cat", "draw a house with a tree", "draw a rocket". The AI (Kimi K3) sketches it and each
  stroke is drawn as soon as it arrives.
- Marking things on screen still works: "draw a box around the volume list", "circle the Save button".
- Drawings stay while Wisp talks, then fade away. Escape clears them.

**Smart routing (NVIDIA).** Quick how-to questions ("how do I open a new tab in Chrome?", "how do I see my
download history?") go to Nemotron, the fast model. Everything else, including explanations, writing help and
drawing, goes to Kimi K3, the smarter one, which takes a couple of seconds longer. Each backs the other up. On by
default; turn it off in Settings > AI to use one model for everything.

Requires macOS 15 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon.
