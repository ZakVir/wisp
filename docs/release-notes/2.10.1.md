## Wisp 2.10.1

**Walkthroughs don't repeat a step you've already done.** If you click the spot Wisp showed and the screen hasn't
caught up yet (a window still opening), Wisp used to tell you to click it again. Now:

- Clicking the highlighted spot, or pressing a key, always counts as doing the step, even when the screen barely
  changes (a checkbox, a toggle).
- The next request tells the AI plainly that you clicked it, so it moves on to the step after it.
- If the AI still only repeats the step, Wisp doesn't say it: it shows "Waiting for that to open…" and looks again
  the moment the screen changes. If you click again and it still says the same step, Wisp says it then (your
  click may not have registered).

Requires macOS 15 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon.
