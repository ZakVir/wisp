## Wisp 0.4.1

**Pause Wisp.** In the Wisp menu: **Pause Wisp ▸ For 1 Hour / Until Tomorrow / Until I Resume**. While paused,
the shortcuts do nothing (a press shows a small reminder), the menu bar icon shows a pause sign, and
**Resume Wisp** is at the top of the menu. A pause survives a restart and ends by itself on time.

Handy for presentations, screen sharing, games and calls.

Requires macOS 15 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon.
