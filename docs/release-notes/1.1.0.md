## Wisp 1.1.0

**Answers in other languages.** Type a question in Spanish, French, German, Japanese… (double-tap Control), or
write "answer in French" under Settings > AI > About you: Wisp answers in that language, and speaks it with the
Mac's own voice for that language (Wisp's own voice speaks English only). English answers sound the same as
before.

Spoken questions are still understood in English only.

In our real-app test, accuracy in English is unchanged (86%).

Requires macOS 15 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon.
