## Wisp 2.5.0

**Second opinion.** If an answer was wrong, hold the shortcut and say "try again", "that's not right", "look
again" or "second opinion". Wisp asks your last question again with a fresh look at the screen, and (with the
free NVIDIA service) uses the other model first: Kimi K3, a bit slower but more careful. You don't have to repeat
the question.

Requires macOS 15 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon.
