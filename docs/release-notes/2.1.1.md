## Wisp 2.1.1

**Dictionary.** "Define ephemeral", "what's the definition of serendipity?", "what does the word ubiquitous
mean?": Wisp says the main meaning right away, from the dictionary built into your Mac (offline, instant), and
shows the full entry in a card. Words the dictionary doesn't know go to the AI as usual.

Requires macOS 15 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon.
