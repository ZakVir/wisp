## Wisp 2.5.6

**Ask your History.** "What did you tell me about cropping earlier?", "what did you say about the Wi-Fi setting?":
Wisp finds the matching answer in History and says it again (on your Mac; nothing is sent). History is kept until
Wisp quits, or longer if you turn on Keep history on this Mac.

Requires macOS 15 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon.
