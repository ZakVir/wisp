## Wisp 2.2.1

**Time and date, anywhere.** "What time is it?", "what's the date?", "what time is it in Tokyo / London / Dubai /
Sydney…?": answered instantly from your Mac's clock (with the day, when it's a different day there). Places Wisp
doesn't know go to the AI as usual.

Requires macOS 15 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon.
