## Wisp 2.10.0

**Step-by-step walkthroughs that wait for you.** Ask "how do I start a new session?" (or any "how do I…"), and
Wisp looks at your screen, tells you the first step and points to it, then watches while you do it:

- **Any way you do the step counts:** a click (anywhere, not just on the exact spot), a keyboard shortcut, Return,
  or typing.
- **It waits for the screen to settle** (a menu opening, a window sliding in, a page loading), takes a fresh look,
  and tells you the next step, pointing again. If nothing changed (a stray click), it keeps waiting.
- **It knows when you're done.** The AI says whether more steps follow, so Wisp stops when the task is finished
  instead of guessing.
- **Talk to it along the way:** say "what now?", "I did it" or "next" for the next step; ask anything else and the
  walkthrough ends. Esc stops it too, and it ends by itself after 3 quiet minutes or 12 steps.
- If a step's answer only points without saying anything, Wisp still says where ("Here: Erase.").

Measured on 58 real questions in real Mac apps: Nemotron 3.5 Super VL 96% right with walkthroughs on (94% before).

Requires macOS 15 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon.
