## What's new in Wisp 0.3.0

**Wisp now updates itself.** It checks once a day and installs updates quietly when you're not using it. You can
turn this off in Settings > About.

### A clearer voice
- Wisp's voice no longer sounds grainy, and it speaks at a natural pace. Answers also start sooner.
- Abbreviations are read properly: "P-D-F", "U-S-B", "mac O S".
- New: turn **Speak answers out loud** off to get captions only.

### Easier to set up, and make it yours
- A new welcome animation, and a setup guide that always fits on screen.
- A **Try it out** page: ask a real question, then pick your shortcut, voice, speed and cursor look right there.
- **Your own cursor skin**: one colour, a gradient, or the colours of any photo.
- Use NVIDIA (free) or **any OpenAI-compatible AI service**, including models running on your own Mac.
- **Replay Welcome** in Settings > General goes through setup again.

### More dependable
- **Stop** in the menu ends whatever Wisp is doing: listening, thinking or speaking.
- If a step gets stuck, Wisp gives up after 10 seconds and says so.
- Plugging in or unplugging headphones mid-answer no longer cuts Wisp off.
- A black screen (protected video, sleeping display) no longer stops questions that aren't about the screen.
- Captions open at the right size, and speech downloads retry by themselves.

Requires macOS 15 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon.
