## Wisp 2.1.0

**Instant math and conversions.** Plain arithmetic and unit conversions are now answered on your Mac: exact,
instant, and even offline.

- "What's 15% of 80?", "what's 128 times 7?", "calculate (2 + 3) × 4", "what's the square root of 81?"
- "Convert 5 miles to kilometers", "72 Fahrenheit in Celsius", "how many ounces in a pound?", "10 km in miles"
  (lengths, weights, temperatures, cooking volumes, speeds)

Anything else still goes to the AI as usual. They're in History too.

Requires macOS 15 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon.
