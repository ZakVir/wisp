## Wisp 0.3.6

**Type a question instead of saying it.** Double-tap Control (or choose **Type a Question…** in the Wisp menu),
type, and press Return. Handy in a quiet office, a meeting, or for long names that are hard to say.

- Wisp looks at your screen the moment the box opens, so the box itself is never in the picture.
- The app you're asking about stays in front; Escape or clicking away closes the box.
- Turn the double-tap off in Settings > General if you use Control taps for something else.

Requires macOS 15 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon.
