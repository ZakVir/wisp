## Wisp 0.3.5

**Recent answers** in the Wisp menu:

- **Say Last Answer Again** repeats the last answer (with captions).
- **Copy Last Answer** puts it on the clipboard, ready to paste.
- **Recent Questions** lists your last 10 questions; click one to copy its answer.
- They're kept only in memory and disappear when Wisp quits. **Clear** removes them right away.

Requires macOS 15 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon.
