## Wisp 2.4.4

**"Stop" just stops.** Holding the shortcut and saying "stop", "stop it", "be quiet", "shh", "that's enough" or
"never mind" now simply stops Wisp, with no reply. (In 1.3.1, "be quiet" also switched Wisp to captions only,
which was a surprise; to do that on purpose, say "captions only" or "stop talking out loud".) This also stops the
AI from replying to "stop it" as if it had done something.

Requires macOS 15 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon.
