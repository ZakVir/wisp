## Wisp 1.2.0

**Copy text on screen.** Say "copy the text on my screen" (or "…in this image", "…from this video"), or choose
**Copy Text on Screen** in the Wisp menu. Wisp reads every bit of text on the display you're using, even text in
pictures, videos and dialogs you can't select, and puts it on the clipboard, ready to paste.

- Done entirely on your Mac with macOS's own text recognition: nothing is sent anywhere.
- Private apps are left out, and so is the menu bar.

Requires macOS 15 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon.
