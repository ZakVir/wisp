## Wisp 2.10.3

**Answers come from what's on your screen.** Asked "how do I start a new session?" with Claude full screen, Wisp
said "New Session in the File menu" and pointed at a hidden menu bar, with the New button right there in the
sidebar. Fixed:

- **The most accurate model answers every question.** Quick how-to questions used to go to the fast model, which
  answered from habit instead of from the screen (4 of 12 right on the Claude test). Now Nemotron 3.5 Super VL
  answers first (12 of 12: "Click the New button at the top of the sidebar"), with the fast model as backup. It
  takes about a second longer.
- **Wisp sees inside apps built on Electron** (Claude, Slack, VS Code, Discord…): it asks them to report their
  buttons as soon as they come to the front, which such apps only do when asked.
- **When an app still won't say what's in its window**, Wisp tells the AI to find things in the screenshot instead
  of handing it a list with only menu names.
- **Full screen:** Wisp knows the menu bar is hidden and doesn't point at it.
- **Walkthroughs end cleanly:** when the AI's last reply only says the task is done, Wisp says "That's it, you're
  all set." (it used to treat that as a broken reply and go quiet).

Requires macOS 15 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon.
