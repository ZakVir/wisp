## Wisp 0.6.0

**Wisp Check-up.** Something not working? Choose **Check Wisp…** in the Wisp menu (or Settings > About). One
window tests every part Wisp needs and shows the button that fixes each problem:

- Microphone, seeing your screen, noticing your shortcut (and whether Wisp is paused)
- Speech recognition and Wisp's voice (both on this Mac)
- The AI service: a live connection test with a blank test image (nothing from your screen is sent)
- Better answers and updates

**Copy Report** copies versions and the results, nothing personal, ready to paste into an email for help.

Also: while the speech model loads after a restart, Wisp now says it's "getting ready" instead of "still
downloading", and cards in What's New are easier to see in light mode.

Requires macOS 15 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon.
