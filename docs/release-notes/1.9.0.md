## Wisp 1.9.0

**Summarize or translate the page.** Writing help now works on the whole window when nothing is selected:

- "summarize this page", "give me the gist of this article", "what are the key points of this email?":
  Wisp reads a short summary aloud (about 50 words) and shows it in a card.
- "translate this page into English": the translation goes in the card and on your clipboard.

The window's text is read on your Mac (only that window, private apps never), then sent to the AI service with
your request. With text selected, only the selection is used, as before.

Requires macOS 15 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon.
