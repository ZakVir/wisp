## Wisp 0.7.0

**Answers you can read.** After a typed question (double-tap Control), the whole answer also appears in a card
right under where you typed, with a **Copy** button. Handy in meetings or with the sound off.

- The card stays while you're reading (it waits while the pointer is over it) and fades on its own; Escape
  or the × closes it.
- It never takes the keyboard away from the app you're using.
- Turn it off in Settings > General > Show typed answers in a card.

Requires macOS 15 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon.
