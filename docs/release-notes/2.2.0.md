## Wisp 2.2.0

**A closer look.** Small print, a tiny chart, a blurry button? Point at it and say "magnify this", "give me a
closer look" or "show me this bigger". Wisp shows the area around your pointer 2.5× bigger, sharp (captured at
your display's full resolution), in a panel over it. Click it or press Escape to close; it fades after 20
seconds.

Done on your Mac; private apps are left out. (Questions like "how do I make it bigger?" are still answered as
before.)

Requires macOS 15 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon.
