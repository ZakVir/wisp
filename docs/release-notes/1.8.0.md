## Wisp 1.8.0

**Timers and reminders.** Hold the shortcut and say:

- "set a timer for 5 minutes" (or "start a 10 minute timer", "set a timer for half an hour")
- "remind me in 20 minutes to call Sam", "remind me to check my email in 2 hours"
- "remind me at 3 pm to send the report" ("at 4:30" means the next 4:30)
- "how much time is left?", "cancel my timer"

When it's time, Wisp plays a chime, says it, and shows a card. Running timers are listed in the Wisp menu, with
**Cancel**. Kept by Wisp itself (no AI, nothing sent); timers end if Wisp quits.

Requires macOS 15 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon.
