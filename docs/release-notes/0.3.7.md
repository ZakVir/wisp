## Wisp 0.3.7

**Wisp knows what "this" is.** With Better answers on (Settings > Permissions), Wisp also reads:

- **the text you've selected**: select a word or a paragraph and ask "what does this mean?", "translate this"
  or "is this right?";
- **the button under your mouse pointer**: point at something and ask "what does this do?".

Privacy: passwords are never read, and text you've typed only goes with a question if you select it. The
explanations in setup and Settings > Privacy now say this.

Requires macOS 15 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon.
