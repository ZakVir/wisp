## Wisp 2.4.0

**Your Mac, at a glance.** Answered straight from your Mac, instantly and offline:

- "How much storage do I have left?" / "Is my disk full?" (with a tip when it's getting low)
- "What's my battery?" (percentage, and whether it's charging)
- "What version of macOS do I have?"
- "How much memory does my Mac have?"
- "How long has my Mac been on?"

Requires macOS 15 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon.
