## Wisp 1.7.0

**Quiet during calls.** When another app is using the microphone (a Zoom, Teams, Meet or FaceTime call, a voice
memo, dictation), Wisp doesn't talk over it: the answer appears as captions and stays in a card with **Copy**,
and Wisp still points. A short note says it's answering quietly.

- Wisp asks macOS which apps are recording right now, so music playing in your headphones doesn't count.
- Turn it off in Settings > General > Answer quietly during calls.

Requires macOS 15 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon.
