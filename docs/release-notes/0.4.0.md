## Wisp 0.4.0

**Step-by-step guidance.** Ask how to do something ("how do I export this as a PDF?"). When Wisp points at the
first step, just click it: Wisp takes a fresh look at your screen and shows the next step, and so on until the
task is done (up to 8 steps).

- Each step is one short sentence and a point.
- Stops by itself when the task is done, when you click somewhere else, after 90 seconds without a click, or
  when you press Escape or ask something new.
- Turn it off in Settings > General > Guide me step by step.

Works with the free NVIDIA models (each step is one quick question).

Requires macOS 15 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon.
