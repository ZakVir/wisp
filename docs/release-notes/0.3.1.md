## Wisp 0.3.1

Polish from a full start-to-finish test:

- Setup now downloads the speech models by itself if they're missing (for example after Remove Models).
- The speech page says "All set" once everything is ready, and "Not downloaded yet" instead of "Waiting to download".
- Settings > About shows Wisp's real icon, even with the Clear or Tinted icon styles.
- The shortcut page lines its controls up neatly and lets you record your own shortcut there too.
- A new installer window: drag Wisp onto Applications.

Requires macOS 15 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon.
