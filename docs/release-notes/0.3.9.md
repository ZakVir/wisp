## Wisp 0.3.9

**About you.** In Settings > AI there's a new box where you can tell Wisp about yourself or how you like answers,
in your own words. For example "I'm new to Macs, explain simply", "keep answers very short" or "I edit videos in
Final Cut Pro for work". Wisp follows it in every answer.

- Quick examples to start from; up to 500 characters.
- What you write goes with each question to the AI service (Settings > Privacy says so when it's filled in).

Requires macOS 15 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon.
