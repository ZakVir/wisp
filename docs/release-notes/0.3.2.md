## Wisp 0.3.2

Better answers:

- General questions (cooking, weather, facts) are answered straight away, without "I can't help with that" or
  "I don't see that on your screen" first.
- When Wisp explains something it can see on your screen ("what's First Aid?"), it now also points at it. Before
  pointing, it checks the name is really written there, using text recognition on your Mac.
- Wisp's thinking can never be read out by accident.
- On the welcome screen, the icon's tile now stands out from the dark window.

In our 200-question test, 1 answer is flagged, down from 9.

Requires macOS 15 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon.
