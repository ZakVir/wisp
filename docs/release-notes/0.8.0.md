## Wisp 0.8.0

**Private apps.** In Settings > Privacy > Private apps, choose apps Wisp should never see, for example a
password manager, a banking app or a patient-records system.

- Their windows are left out of every screenshot (macOS removes them before Wisp gets the picture), on every
  display.
- Their buttons, menus and selected text are never read, and Read Aloud skips them.
- If you ask about a private app, Wisp says it doesn't look at that app instead of guessing.
- **Add Password Apps** adds the password managers installed on your Mac in one click.
- Companies can set this list for everyone (managed settings).

Requires macOS 15 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon.
