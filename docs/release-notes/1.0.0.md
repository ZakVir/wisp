## Wisp 1.0.0

**History.** Choose **History…** in the Wisp menu to see your questions and Wisp's answers, newest first, with
search, **Copy**, **Say Again** and **Delete**. Spoken and typed questions are marked, with the app you were in.

- By default History is kept only until Wisp quits.
- Turn on **Keep history on this Mac** (in History, or Settings > Privacy) to keep it between launches: text
  only, saved on this Mac, never screenshots or audio. Turning it off deletes the saved file.

**Wisp 1.0.** Since 0.3.2: Better answers (reads the app's buttons, menus and your selection), typed questions
with an answer card, step-by-step guidance, read aloud, "say that again", the keyboard shortcuts card, private
apps, About you and answer length, Pause, Escape to stop, the Check-up, What's New, and many small fixes. The
getting-started guide below covers all of it.

Requires macOS 15 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon.
