## Wisp 2.2.2

**Your Wisp.** Settings > About now shows how many questions Wisp has answered (and how many this week), how
quickly it usually starts talking after you let go, and how many it answered right on your Mac without the AI.
Worked out from the timing measurements kept on this Mac (no content); turn those off in Settings > Privacy.

Requires macOS 15 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon.
