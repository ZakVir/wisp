## Wisp 2.3.1

The getting-started guide (below, and on every release page) now covers everything added since 1.0: quick
actions, writing help and page summaries, instant answers on your Mac (math, conversions, time, definitions,
timers, notes), a closer look, copying text on screen, quiet answers during calls, and caption position.

Requires macOS 15 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon.
