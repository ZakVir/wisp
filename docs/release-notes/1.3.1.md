## Wisp 1.3.1

**Change how Wisp talks, by asking.** Hold the shortcut and say:

- "talk faster" / "speak slower" (a step at a time) or "normal speed";
- "be quiet" (or "captions only"): answers appear as captions without sound;
- "turn your voice back on".

Wisp confirms, and the change shows in Settings > General too. No AI needed.

Requires macOS 15 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon.
