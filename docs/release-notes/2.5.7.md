## Wisp 2.5.7

**"What can you do?"** Wisp now answers from what this version really can do (reading aloud, writing help,
summaries, instant answers, timers, notes and more), quickly and the same way every time, and shows a card of
examples to try. (This is also the first question in setup.)

Requires macOS 15 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon.
