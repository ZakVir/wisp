## Wisp 2.10.2

**No more "you're on a call" when you aren't.** Wisp answers quietly (a card instead of speaking) while another app
is using the microphone for a call. macOS keeps the microphone open in the background for "Hey Siri" and dictation,
and Wisp counted that as a call, so on some Macs every answer came as a silent card. macOS's own background
listeners (Siri, dictation, Sound Recognition, Voice Control) no longer count; Zoom, FaceTime, Teams, Discord and
calls in a browser still do.

Requires macOS 15 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon.
