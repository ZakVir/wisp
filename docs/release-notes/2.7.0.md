## Wisp 2.7.0

**Wisp can search the web for you.** Hold the shortcut and say:

- "Go online and search for best pizza in Austin", "search the web for …", "google …", "look up … online"
- "Open YouTube", "take me to Gmail", "go to apple.com" (and Google Maps, Drive, Docs, Wikipedia, Amazon, Netflix,
  GitHub, Reddit, LinkedIn, X, ChatGPT, Claude, Outlook, Spotify, news, weather)

You see the cursor do it: it flies to your browser in the Dock and clicks, moves to the address bar and types,
and the page opens in a **new tab** of your default browser (Safari, Chrome, Edge, Arc, Brave or Firefox). With
Better answers on, the cursor goes exactly to the Dock icon and the address bar.

Wisp only ever opens searches and websites this way: it doesn't click or type anything else in your apps. The
search goes to Google. Turn it off in Settings > General > Let Wisp search and open websites.

Requires macOS 15 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon.
